Teaching philosophy

Clear explanations, practical examples, and reproducible materials help learners connect concepts with real applications.

Learning objectives

The materials in this section are designed to help learners:

  1. Understand important statistical and analytical concepts.
  2. Connect mathematical ideas with practical applications.
  3. Apply methods using reproducible computational workflows.
  4. Interpret results carefully and communicate conclusions clearly.
  5. Build the confidence needed to explore more advanced topics independently.

Prerequisites and software

Prerequisites depend on the course or tutorial. Each teaching page will identify the background knowledge required before beginning.

When computation is involved, materials may use:

  • R and RStudio for statistical analysis
  • Quarto for reproducible documents and presentations
  • Course-specific R packages and datasets

Installation instructions and required software will be listed on the relevant page.
